Privacy Policy for WED30APR

Last updated: April 30, 2025

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data.

The extension operates entirely within the user's browser to analyze webpage content for stock tickers and retrieve publicly available YouTube videos related to those tickers.

No personal data is collected, stored, or shared with any third parties.

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact your@email.com.
